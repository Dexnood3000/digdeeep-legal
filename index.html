DigDeeep

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

DigDeeep counts your push-ups using the front camera, entirely on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not collect.

The short version: your camera feed and body-pose analysis never leave your phone — no image, frame, or video is ever stored or transmitted. The only information that leaves your device is your sign-in identifier (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) and your purchase status, used solely to run your account and subscription, plus — on iPhone only — optional, pseudonymous usage analytics that you can turn off in Settings. The Android app has no analytics. There are no ads and no cross-app tracking.

Who we are

DigDeeep is developed by David Roberts ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact hkdavidr@icloud.com.

Camera and push-up counting

DigDeeep uses your device's front camera to detect body pose and count repetitions in real time — Apple's Vision framework on iPhone, Google's MediaPipe pose landmarker on Android. Both run entirely on your device.

  • No photo, video frame, or camera image is ever saved to disk or sent off the device.
  • No pose or skeleton data is transmitted anywhere.
  • Camera access is used only while a session is active, and only after you grant permission.

Information we collect

  • Your sign-in identifier. On iPhone, Sign in with Apple provides a stable user identifier; on Android, Sign in with Google provides one. We use it as your account key and to anchor your free-trial and subscription state so they follow your account across devices and reinstalls. If you choose to share your name, we store only a first name to personalize the app. The iPhone and Android apps hold separate accounts — neither is linked to the other, and a subscription bought on one does not transfer to the other.
  • Purchase and subscription status. Whether you are on a trial, free, or paid plan, and which product you bought. This is handled through Apple or Google Play, and RevenueCat (see below).
  • Usage analytics (iPhone only). The iPhone app sends pseudonymous usage analytics through TelemetryDeck. These are product-interaction signals: which screens are viewed, when sessions start and finish (with the mode and rep counts), onboarding steps, paywall views, purchase outcomes, daily-task progress, and counts describing how well the rep counter is tracking. Each signal also carries coarse context: your subscription tier, days since install, time of day, and weekday. Signals are tied to a salted, one-way hash of your Apple ID identifier, computed on your device (and hashed again by the TelemetryDeck SDK) — never the raw identifier, your name, email, age, sex, or any text you type. Before you sign in, a random anonymous per-install ID is used instead. Camera frames, images, video, and pose data are never part of these signals. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data; turning it off stops all sending immediately. The Android app has no analytics.

Information stored only on your device

Your profile (age and biological sex, used to estimate your fitness ranking) and your workout history (rep counts, dates, streaks, and personal bests) are stored locally on your device — in SwiftData on iPhone, in a Room database on Android. This information is not uploaded to us.

Third-party services

We rely on four providers, all used strictly to operate and improve the app:

  • Apple (iPhone) — for Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Android) — for Sign in with Google and Google Play purchases. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — to validate purchases and manage entitlements and trial state. It receives your sign-in identifier and purchase information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • TelemetryDeck (iPhone) — privacy-focused analytics provider that receives the pseudonymous usage signals described above, and nothing else from the app. It can be turned off in Settings. See TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not show a tracking prompt.
  • No collection of health, photo, video, or camera data.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention and deletion

Your on-device data remains until you delete it or delete the app.

  • Android. Settings → Delete account & data erases every session, streak, record and total on the phone and signs you out.
  • iPhone. Revoke DigDeeep from Settings → your Apple ID → Sign-In & Security, then delete the app.
  • Either way, you can email hkdavidr@icloud.com and we will remove the account data our providers hold — your sign-in identifier and purchase records.

Full instructions are on the data deletion page. Note that Apple and Google retain transaction records independently under their own policies, and that deleting your data does not cancel a subscription — cancel that in the App Store or Google Play.

Children

DigDeeep is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. To exercise any of these, contact hkdavidr@icloud.com.
