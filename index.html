DigDeeep

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 July 2026

DigDeeep counts your push-ups using the front camera, entirely on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not collect.

The short version: your camera feed and body-pose analysis never leave your iPhone — no image, frame, or video is ever stored or transmitted. The only information that leaves your device is your Sign in with Apple identifier and your purchase status, used solely to run your account and subscription. There are no analytics, ad, or tracking tools of any kind.

Who we are

DigDeeep is developed by David Roberts ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact davidr1403@icloud.com.

Camera and push-up counting

DigDeeep uses your device's front camera and Apple's on-device Vision framework to detect body pose and count repetitions in real time. This all happens locally on your iPhone.

  • No photo, video frame, or camera image is ever saved to disk or sent off the device.
  • No pose or skeleton data is transmitted anywhere.
  • Camera access is used only while a session is active, and only after you grant permission.

Information we collect

  • Sign in with Apple identifier. When you sign in, Apple provides a stable user identifier. We use it as your account key and to anchor your free-trial and subscription state so they follow your Apple ID across devices and reinstalls. If you choose to share your name, we store only a first name to personalize the app.
  • Purchase and subscription status. Whether you are on a trial, free, or paid plan, and which product you bought. This is handled through Apple and RevenueCat (see below).

Information stored only on your device

Your profile (age and biological sex, used to estimate your fitness ranking) and your workout history (rep counts, dates, streaks, and personal bests) are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. This information is not uploaded to us.

Third-party services

We rely on two providers, both used strictly to operate the app:

  • Apple — for Sign in with Apple and App Store purchases. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — to validate purchases and manage entitlements and trial state. It receives your Apple sign-in identifier and purchase information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • No collection of health, photo, video, or camera data.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention and deletion

Your on-device data remains until you delete the app. To delete the account data held by our providers — your Sign in with Apple identifier and purchase records — revoke DigDeeep from Settings → your Apple ID → Sign-In & Security, or email davidr1403@icloud.com and we will remove it. Note that Apple retains transaction records independently under its own policies.

Children

DigDeeep is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. To exercise any of these, contact davidr1403@icloud.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
